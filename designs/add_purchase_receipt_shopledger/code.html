Help & Error Recovery Guide

Simple, non-technical answers for common counter bill scanning scenarios in your store.

videocam_off

Camera permission was denied

Browser blocked video access

Camera was accidentally blocked. Look at the top lock icon in your browser address bar (🔒), tap it, change Camera to "Allow", and refresh.

Quick workaround: Use "Upload a photo" or snap the bill using your phone's normal camera app and send to WhatsApp web.
blur_on

Photo is too blurry or dark

OCR low confidence warning

We had trouble reading the faint purple carbon print or small thermal ink numbers. Turn on your counter tube light or tap the torch/flash icon in the camera viewfinder.

Pro Tip: Place the bill on a flat dark counter surface so the white paper borders pop with sharp contrast.
file_present

File is larger than 15 MB

Size limit exceeded

Very high-res RAW camera files take too long over 4G shop connections. WhatsApp-compressed bill photos (typically 1-3 MB) or standard phone photos work fastest and scan in under 2 seconds.

wifi_off

Internet connection dropped

Safe offline queue enabled

Don't worry, your bill photo is saved securely inside your browser storage right now. As soon as your Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot reconnects, ShopLedger will automatically resume processing.
